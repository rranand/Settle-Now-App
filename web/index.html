About Settle Now

Need a way to split bills equally among friends/flatmates? Then don't worry, we have a solution for you. Let's make it possible using "Settle Now".

Settle Now helps to split daily bills within your friends, roommates, flatmates, etc, and eliminate the stressing about “who owes who.”

By using the Settle Now, we can efficiently manage all costs per individual, track ‘who paid how much and ‘who should pay to whom’ via your mobile devices.

Our App Feature

  • Create groups for any splitting situation.

  • All expenses are backed-up online so anyone can log in, view their balances, and add expenses in the room.

  • Can track your personal expense and can categorise among different categories and can analyse using a graph.

  • Can check your previous expense, under the closed room section after everyone has paid the amount.

  • Track your all bank transcations in one place

  • Activity feed and push notifications to help you stay on top of changes.

  • Set remainder such that you will be reminded every month for rent, bill payment, etc on time
